Yanjie Zhang

I study how AI agents resolve conflicting information: when a user retracts an earlier instruction, when memory disagrees with what the agent now observes, when what is heard disagrees with the transcript, or when a chart's visuals contradict its data.

I am an MSc student in Big Data Technology at HKUST and a Machine Learning Research Intern at NVIDIA NVAITC, collaborating with the Healthcare AI Team on medical AI and Nemotron post-training. Previously, I worked at Tencent Games and Binance (AI Core Contributor).

I am open to AI research and algorithm roles. Feel free to reach out!
